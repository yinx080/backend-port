Drop your about-page photo in this folder.

1. Name it whatever you like, e.g. portrait.jpg
2. Put that filename in about.py -> PORTRAIT
3. The page frames it at 4:5, so a portrait-orientation crop works best.
   Around 1200x1500 px is plenty.

Until the file named in PORTRAIT exists here, the page shows a labelled
empty frame instead of a broken image.

If you REPLACE the photo later without renaming it, bump VERSION in main.py
so browsers fetch the new one instead of the cached one.

You can delete this note once your photo is in.
